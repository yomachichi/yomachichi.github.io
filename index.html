Accessible PDF rebuilds

We rebuild your PDF as a new tagged PDF/UA-1 file aimed at WCAG 2.1 AA: headings, reading order, tables with column headers, alt text, title, language and bookmarks, with a veraPDF check report for every file. The words and order stay the same; the layout becomes a clean template, not a copy of your design.

Price · email for a quote

Send the files and we reply with a page count and a fixed price. Not a fit: keeping your exact original design, forms with dropdowns, radio buttons or signatures, tables with row headers, multi-column layouts, languages other than English. Not a guarantee of legal compliance.

How to buy

  1. Email team@yomachichi.com with what you need. Using the product name in the subject (for example token migration, operator pack, guardrail review, accessible PDF) gets it to the right place fastest.
  2. We reply with a quote: what is included, what is not, and the price.
  3. When you are happy, we send a secure Shopify invoice you can pay by card or Shop Pay. For the token migration the invoice comes only after the acceptance test passes on your development store.

Never send payment details, passwords, API secrets or tokens by email. Payment only ever happens on the Shopify invoice page, and we will never ask for your keys.
